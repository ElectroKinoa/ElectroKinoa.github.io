# ElectroKinoa

**Solar-powered light messages embedded in concrete.**

Street artist who turns urban concrete into a quiet, self-powered canvas. Each piece carries a luminous message that charges in the sun by day and glows when night falls, with no wiring and no outlet needed.

---

## What I do

I create **solar-powered light messages integrated directly into concrete**. Small solar cells and LEDs are cast into the material itself, so the words become part of the wall.

I use this technique in two ways:

### 1. Crack repairs
Cracks in walls, pavements and urban structures are usually patched up and forgotten. I fill them with concrete that carries an embedded luminous message, turning damage into a visible, glowing statement. The repair stays in place and speaks for itself after dark.

### 2. Bas-reliefs on façades
I cast concrete bas-reliefs with integrated light messages, then fix them onto building façades. Each piece adds texture, volume and light to the architecture, and works as a small sculpture as well as a message.

---

## How it works

- **Solar energy**: built-in photovoltaic cells collect light during the day.
- **Light at night**: stored energy powers LEDs that display the message once it gets dark.
- **Concrete as a medium**: the electronics are cast into the concrete, which is both the structure and the artwork.
- **Autonomous**: no cables, no external power source, no maintenance.

---

## Themes

- Urban repair and resilience
- Visibility of what is usually ignored (cracks, neglected walls)
- Technology and raw materials working together
- Public space as a place for quiet, persistent messages
